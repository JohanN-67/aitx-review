**Core Objective:** Develop a predictive model for donor‑recipient matching to improve six‑month graft survival after lung transplantation.

**Patient Cohort & Study Scope:** 404 adult lung transplant recipients (mean age not reported) undergoing single, bilateral or bilobular transplants at Reina Sofía University Hospital, Córdoba, Spain, from October 1993 to January 2016.

**AI/ML Approach:** Hybrid logistic‑regression with product‑unit neural network (LRIPU) combining evolutionary product‑unit neural networks (EPUNN) and traditional logistic regression; input variables = 36 donor/recipient clinical features (tabular EMR data).

**Key Findings:** The LRIPU model (48 parameters) identified key positive predictors of six‑month survival: pre‑ and post‑transplant FVC (coeff 23.50, 3.03), lower pre‑transplant PCO₂ (3.12). Negative predictors included pre‑transplant FEV₁ (−23.51), older recipient age (2.16), donor age (1.55), longer cold ischemia time, need for surgical tailoring, cardiopulmonary bypass, and certain donor death causes. Model performance metrics such as AUC, accuracy, or C‑statistic were **Not explicitly reported**; authors state it “exceeded the capacity of classical statistical methods.”

**Main Limitations:** Retrospective single‑center design without external validation.
